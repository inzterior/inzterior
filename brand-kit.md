## Proposed Trust Framework (confirm before publishing — this is a real commitment, not just marketing language)

1. **Staged/milestone payments** — small design deposit (~10%) to start; further payments released only against completed milestones (design sign-off → materials/carpentry → on-site installation → handover). No large upfront sums for work not yet done.
2. **Written contract** covering scope, materials, and timeline before any work begins beyond the design deposit.
3. **Defects warranty** — a fixed period (suggest 6 months) after handover covering workmanship issues.
4. **Progress transparency** — photo/video updates at each milestone, not just at the end.
5. **Visible legal identity** — SSM Reg. No. SA0647003-M and the real Horizon Hills address on every contract and invoice (already true on the website footer).

## Company Story

Malaysia's interior design industry has a trust problem. Homeowners hand over five-figure deposits to contractors who disappear, half-finished renovations, designers who were never properly registered in the first place — the stories are common enough that many people delay renovating altogether, just to avoid the risk.

Inzterior was started by Billy Yeap to be the opposite of that. Every project runs on a written contract before a ringgit changes hands beyond a small design deposit. Payments are staged to match actual progress — clients pay for what's been built, not what's been promised. And the studio itself, Istory Design Studio (SSM Reg. No. SA0647003-M), is a real, registered business with a real address in Horizon Hills, Iskandar Puteri — not just a Facebook page and a WhatsApp number.

None of this makes for the flashiest marketing. It's slower to build a client base this way than promising the moon in an ad. But it means that when someone hires Inzterior, they're not gambling — they're working with a studio that has as much to lose from cutting corners as they do.
