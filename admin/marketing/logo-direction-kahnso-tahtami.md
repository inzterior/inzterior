# Logo Direction Brief — Kahnso & Tahtami

*iStory group · Draft v1 · 2026-09-11 · for designer handoff*
*Companion to: brand-story-kahnso-tahtami.md*

These are **directions, not final logos**. The concept sketches only show the idea. A designer should refine proportions, custom-draw the letterforms and test them at small sizes.

---

## Shared system rules (both brands)

1. **Mark + wordmark lockup.** Each logo has a symbol (for app icons, profile pictures and stamps) and a wordmark, usable together or apart.
2. **Endorsement line:** "AN ISTORY COMPANY" in small tracked caps under the wordmark, for formal uses such as letterheads, invoices, signage and the showroom. Leave it off social avatars.
3. **Sister-brand link:** Inzterior is black and gold. Neither new brand should copy that, but both may use **one gold hairline or detail** in premium applications (showroom signage, name cards) to show they belong to the same group.
4. **Must work in one colour** for laser etching (Kahnso), embossing, rubber stamps and invoices.
5. **Minimum size test:** the mark must still read at 32 px (favicon) and as a 110 px circular crop (Instagram/Facebook avatar).

# KAHNSO — "The groove"

**Idea:** *kanso*, simplicity. The logo should look like it has had everything removed except one essential line.

## Recommended concept: K1 · The groove ⭐
A solid brushed-steel square (a cabinet door) with **one horizontal cut near the top**. That is the J-pull groove of a handle-less cabinet, the signature detail of minimalist kitchens.
- Why it works: anyone who has seen a modern kitchen recognises it, it's literally what the product is, and it's as simple as a logo can get.
- Bonus use: the groove can be **laser-etched or CNC-cut into real cabinet panels** as a discreet maker's mark, so the product carries its own logo.

## Alternates
- **K2 · Split-K monogram:** a K built from three separate strokes, with the lower leg in lighter steel to suggest reflection. More typographic, and works well embossed.
- **K3 · Hanko seal:** a square seal with 簡 (*kan*, the first character of kanso). Very Japanese and premium, but less readable to non-Chinese readers. Best kept as a **secondary stamp** for warranty cards and packaging rather than the main logo.

## Wordmark
- **All caps, wide geometric sans, generous letter-spacing:** K A H N S O.
- Reference typefaces (Google Fonts, free, for exploration only): *Jost*, *Outfit*, *Michroma* (wider/techier). Final should be custom-adjusted.
- Detail to explore: keep the letterforms very even so the "H" (the added letter) sits naturally and doesn't look like a typo.

## Colour palette
| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Brushed steel | `#B9BDC1` |
| Primary dark | Charcoal | `#2B2D2F` |
| Background | Off-white | `#F4F4F2` |
| Deep | Graphite | `#1F2124` |
| Premium accent (sparingly) | Inzterior gold | *match Inzterior's gold* |

**Finish on print/signage:** brushed-metal foil or real etched stainless for the showroom sign. Avoid chrome/mirror gradients in digital; keep it flat.

## Avoid
Chrome bevels, fake metal gradients, sparkles, kitchen clip-art (knives, pots, flames), heavy "industrial" stencils (reads as canteen), red rising-sun motifs (cliché and politically loaded).

# TAHTAMI — "The 4.5-mat pinwheel"

**Idea:** *tatami*, the floor you live on. The logo should feel warm and natural, and look like an actual floor.

## Recommended concept: T1 · The 4.5-mat pinwheel ⭐
Four rectangular mats arranged in a pinwheel around **one half-mat square in the centre**. This is the classic 4.5-*jō* room layout of Japanese tea rooms. It's authentic, geometric and reads as a floor plan.
- Why it works: it's a real tatami layout (strong story), it looks like floorboards or tiles to anyone who doesn't know the reference, and the centre square gives an accent spot for the green.
- Bonus use: the pinwheel repeats as a **brand pattern** for packaging, sample boxes, showroom floor decals and website backgrounds.

## Alternates
- **T2 · Staggered planks:** three rows of offset planks, i.e. how vinyl planks are actually laid. Very clear for flooring, but more generic, since many flooring brands use planks.
- **T3 · Woven-edge wordmark:** wordmark only, with a double line under it echoing the fabric border (*heri*) on a tatami mat. Elegant, and best for premium print.

## Wordmark
- **Lowercase, warm serif or soft humanist sans:** "tahtami". Lowercase feels friendly and homely, the opposite of Kahnso's caps, so the two brands stand apart side by side.
- Reference typefaces (exploration only): *Fraunces*, *Cormorant*, *Lora* (serif); *Nunito*, *Quicksand* (soft sans).
- Detail to explore: the two "a"s and "h" can share a rounded rhythm, like woven rush.

## Colour palette
| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Tatami straw | `#C9B98A` |
| Accent | Igusa green (the rush tatami is woven from) | `#5E6B4E` |
| Warm | Oak | `#A67B5B` |
| Background | Linen | `#F3EEE4` |
| Deep | Moss charcoal | `#3E4535` |

**Photography mood:** soft daylight, bare feet, children on the floor, linen and plants. Never cold showroom lighting.

## Avoid
Cartoon feet, water-splash "waterproof" graphics in the logo (save them for ads), glossy 3D planks, generic house-roof icons, bamboo clichés.
