# Review-Request Ritual

> Operational process, not a webpage. Defines when and how every client is asked for a Google review after handover, working toward the TASKS.md §6 target of 30 dated, photo-attached reviews by end of 2026.

Last updated: 2026-08-14

---

## Where this plugs into the existing process

This attaches to **Milestone 5 — Handover** (the final payment stage already defined on the site: "the final payment, due at practical completion and handover of the finished space"). No new client-facing step is being introduced — this just adds a review ask onto a moment that already happens on every project.

## The two-touch model

**Touch 1 — At the handover walkthrough (Day 0), verbal, no link.**
During the final walkthrough, once the client has seen the finished space and any punch-list items are either resolved or scheduled, mention it in person or via WhatsApp voice note — not scripted, just natural:

> "If you're happy with how this turned out, it'd really help us if you left a Google review once you've settled in — I'll send you the link in a couple of weeks once you've had a chance to live in it."

This plants the idea while sentiment is highest, without asking for the actual action yet. **Skip this touch entirely if the client seems unhappy or punch-list items are unresolved** — resolve the issue first (that's what the 6-month warranty is for), and don't ask for a review until confidence is back. A review asked for at the wrong moment can turn a neutral client into a negative one.

**Touch 2 — 7–14 days after handover, WhatsApp, with the direct link.**
Waiting past Day 0 matters for two reasons: the client has had time to actually live in the space (more credible review) and time to furnish/style it (better photos). Send:

> Hi [Name], hope you're settling in well! If you have 2 minutes, we'd really appreciate a Google review — it helps other homeowners find us the same way you did. Here's the link: https://g.page/r/CdhhwY68WAFgEBM/review
>
> If you can, snap a photo or two of the finished space when you leave the review — photo reviews are the most useful for people still deciding.

**Touch 3 — one reminder only, Day 21, if nothing yet.**
```
Hi [Name], just a gentle nudge on the review whenever you get a moment — no rush at all. Thanks again for trusting us with the project! https://g.page/r/CdhhwY68WAFgEBM/review
```
**Never send more than one reminder.** Past this, let it go — the trust-first positioning this business runs on is worth more than a review count, and repeated asks read as exactly the kind of pressure this studio positions itself against.

## Hard rule: no incentives

**Never offer a discount, credit, gift, or any form of compensation in exchange for a review.** This isn't a brand-voice preference — it's a Google review policy violation that risks the listing being suspended or the reviews themselves being stripped, which would undo the entire point of this ritual. If a referral/loyalty mechanic gets built later (TASKS.md §3, "referral mechanic drafted"), keep it entirely separate from and never conditioned on leaving a review.

## Language

Default to English for the templates above — matches how client communication already happens per the site's copy. If a client's own conversation has been in Mandarin or Malay, mirror that for the review ask rather than switching to English (same instinct as the RedNote bilingual approach in the brand-voice skill) — natural language match reads as more genuine than a templated one.

## Tracking

Every ask and outcome gets logged in `admin/marketing/review-log.md` (one row per project) — trigger date, both touch dates, and whether/when a review landed. This is what makes the 30-by-end-of-2026 target checkable rather than a vibe.
