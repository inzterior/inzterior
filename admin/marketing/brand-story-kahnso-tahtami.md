# KAHNSO — Stainless Steel Cabinets

**Pronounced:** KAHN-so · **Root:** 簡素 *kanso*, "simplicity"
**Domain:** kahnso.com (available as of 2026-09-11)

## Brand essence
> Simplicity, engineered in steel.

## Short story (bio, packaging, ~50 words)

In Japanese design, *kanso* means removing everything that isn't needed until only the essential remains. Kahnso brings that idea to the Malaysian kitchen: stainless steel cabinets with clean lines, honest material and nothing extra. No swelling, no termites, no compromise. Just a kitchen that stays beautiful for decades.

## Long story (website "About", ~200 words)

Kanso is one of the seven principles of Zen aesthetics. It is the discipline of simplicity: taking away the clutter, the ornament and the excess until what is left is calm, clear and useful.

We built Kahnso on that principle, and on a problem every Malaysian homeowner knows. Our climate is hard on kitchens. Humidity swells particleboard. Termites find their way into timber. Doors sag, laminates peel, and a kitchen that looked perfect on handover day looks tired within a few years.

Stainless steel doesn't have those problems. It doesn't absorb water, it gives termites nothing to eat, and it cleans back to new with a wipe. It is the material professional kitchens trust, and we believe it belongs in the home too, made with the care of fine furniture rather than the look of a canteen.

Every Kahnso cabinet is designed with restraint: precise edges, quiet hardware and finishes chosen to last rather than to follow a trend. Fewer things, made better.

Kahnso is part of the iStory group, alongside Inzterior Interior Design, so every kitchen is planned as part of the whole home, not a catalogue box.

*Simplicity, in steel.*

## Founder note — "Why we built Kahnso"

*Market-pain version (chosen over a personal anecdote). Approved 2026-09-12.*

> **COPY GUARDRAIL — read before writing anything for these brands.**
> Inzterior, Kahnso and Tahtami all launched in 2026. **No copy may claim years of experience, a track record, "established", "since 20XX", numbers of past projects, or testimonials we do not have.** A new brand cannot borrow credibility it has not earned; it earns trust by being verifiable instead. Substitute proof for tenure: show the cabinet carcass, name the steel grade, publish the warranty terms, put a cut-away unit in the showroom.

> **Why we built Kahnso**
>
> In Malaysia, kitchens are sold by the door and priced by the foot. Homeowners are shown finishes and handles, and almost never told what the cabinet body is actually made of. It is usually particleboard or MDF, and that is exactly where kitchens fail: the base cabinet under the sink swells, the plinth softens, termites find the timber, and by year five a kitchen that looked perfect at handover is quietly falling apart behind its pretty doors.
>
> We are a new company, so we will not tell you how long we have been doing this. We will tell you what we changed: the part nobody looks at. Kahnso cabinets are stainless steel throughout, not only on the surface. Nothing to swell, nothing for termites to eat, nothing hidden. Come and open the doors, look under the sink, check the joints. Judge the carcass, not the showroom.
>
> *We show you the inside first.*

**The five market pain points this is built on** (all verifiable by customers, no statistics claimed):
1. Kitchens are sold by the door and priced by the foot; the carcass material is rarely disclosed.
2. Wok cooking, steam, daily washing and year-round humidity attack the sink base cabinet first.
3. Termites are a real local risk to timber and plywood carcasses.
4. The usual durable alternative, aluminium, often feels cold and hardware-like rather than like furniture.
5. Long warranties outlive the sub-contractors who gave them.

**"We show you the inside first"** is strong enough to work as a campaign line in its own right: cut-away cabinet displays in the showroom, and social content showing the carcass before the door.

## Voice
- **Calm, precise, few words.** Short sentences. Let white space and material photos carry it.
- **Confident, not loud.** No "BEST PRICE!!!" and no exclamation marks. Premium brands understate.
- **Proof over adjectives.** Say "304-grade stainless" or "no particleboard", not "super high quality".
- **Words to use:** essential, precise, quiet, enduring, honest material
- **Avoid:** cheap, promo, murah, "industrial" (it reads as canteen)

# TAHTAMI — Vinyl / SPC Flooring

**Pronounced:** tah-TAH-mee · **Root:** 畳 *tatami*, the traditional Japanese floor mat
**Domain:** tahtami.com (available as of 2026-09-11)

## Brand essence
> The floor you live on, barefoot.

## Short story (bio, packaging, ~50 words)

For over a thousand years, Japanese homes were built around the tatami, a floor made to be lived on: sat on, slept on, walked barefoot. Tahtami is the modern tatami for Malaysian homes. Waterproof, quiet, warm underfoot and beautifully natural-looking. A floor made for how we actually live.

## Long story (website "About", ~200 words)

In Japan, rooms were once measured not in square feet but in tatami, the woven mats that covered the floor. Tatami made the floor the heart of the home: where families sat, ate, rested and played, always barefoot.

Malaysian homes share that instinct. We take our shoes off at the door. Our children crawl, play and nap on the floor. Yet many of us live on floors that are cold, hard, noisy or slippery when wet.

Tahtami reimagines the tatami for today. Our vinyl and SPC floors bring the look of natural timber and stone, with the comfort that tiles can't offer: softer and warmer underfoot, quieter to walk on, and fully waterproof for our humid climate, wet kitchens and rainy seasons.

Many of our floors click-lock straight over existing tiles, so the upgrade comes without hacking, dust or weeks of disruption.

Tahtami is part of the iStory group, alongside Inzterior Interior Design and Kahnso, so your floor is chosen as part of the whole home.

*The modern tatami.*

> **Claim check before publishing:** confirm with the supplier which ranges are 100% waterproof, installable over tiles, and their wear-layer and warranty figures. Only publish specs you can back up.

## Voice
- **Warm, homely, sensory.** Talk about feel: warm, soft, quiet, barefoot. It is friendlier than Kahnso.
- **Family-centred.** Kids, grandparents, pets and home life are the heroes of the imagery.
- **Practical reassurance.** Waterproof, easy clean, fast install. Address the real worries.
- **Words to use:** barefoot, warm, quiet, natural, everyday, home
