## 1. Is the research sufficient to build this plan?

Short answer: **yes for strategy and execution, no for hard numbers — and that split is exactly where the plan draws the line.**

**Sufficient — acting on this now:**
- Positioning and messaging (the five white-space gaps, the anti-angles, the "terms before quote" wedge)
- Content and platform strategy (what to post where, in what format, and why — IG/FB/TikTok/XHS)
- Portfolio/case-study restructuring (constraint → decisions → timeline → cost split → revisit)
- The list and priority order of trust assets to build (worksheet, checklist, cost-anatomy page, sample contract, GBP reviews)
- What *not* to do (fear marketing, competitor call-outs, awards-chasing, unverifiable claims)

**Not sufficient — these need real inputs, not more research:**
- **Exact pricing** — the research is a plausibility check against Klang Valley and Singapore numbers, explicitly not a calibration. It has zero Johor-specific psf data. Real calibration needs Billy's actual completed-project costs.
- **Warranty duration** — currently 6 months. The research flags this as weaker than Singapore's 12-month CaseTrust benchmark and recommends a split-by-trade structure. That's a liability decision, not a content decision — it has to come from Billy (and you, on risk appetite).
- **Deposit math** — what the ~10% design deposit is a percentage *of* isn't resolved (see the Terms page work from earlier today). This affects cash flow, not just messaging.
- **A real sample contract** — needs actual legal drafting, not AI-generated boilerplate presented as a binding document.
- **Operational capacity** — the plan recommends commitments like "photo updates within 48 hours of each milestone" and a review-request ritual at every handover. Whether the studio can actually hold that at 2–3x current job volume is something only Billy knows.
- **Jackie's role** — can't slot into the plan, the team page, or any founder-facing content until I know what it is.

None of this blocks starting the plan — it defines Phase 0.

## 5. KPIs — what "working" looks like, checked monthly

In the order the research recommends (leading indicators before vanity metrics):

1. **Sample-contract and worksheet downloads** (once live)
2. **Google review count and recency** — target 30 by end of Phase 3
3. **Share of enquiries that reference the Terms page** ("I saw your payment schedule") — the clearest signal the positioning is landing
4. **Referral share of enquiries**
5. **Time from first contact to signed contract**
6. Follower counts are explicitly **not** a primary KPI — the research's own example (Renozone: 1,358 followers, strong pipeline) shows why.

## 6. What "won Johor" means by 31 Dec 2026 — and what doesn't happen yet

**Realistic by Dec 31, if Phase 0–3 execute on schedule:**
- The only firm in JB with a published payment schedule, warranty scope, and legal-identity page
- 30+ real Google reviews when the nearest JB competitor has none surfaced at all
- 3 fully-documented case studies in a format no competitor uses
- A live, working pricing calculator on real data instead of placeholders
- Content presence on all 4 priority channels, not just launched but running on a steady cadence
- A trademark filing in motion

**Not realistic by Dec 31, and I'd rather say so now than imply it:**
- Measurable "market share" in any quantifiable sense — that requires sales data over a longer window than 5 months
- Expansion into Klang Valley or a dedicated Singapore-buyer funnel — this plan is deliberately Johor-only; doing both at once dilutes the trust-infrastructure advantage that's the actual edge
- The "one year later" revisit content, unless a project already exists that's old enough — this can't be accelerated

**What Dec 31's position sets up for 2027:** once Johor's trust-infrastructure gap is actually closed and documented (not just claimed), the same asset set — worksheet, checklist, case-study format, review base — is the exact thing that ports to a Klang Valley or Singapore-buyer push. This plan is building the reusable proof, not just Johor-specific content.
